# Deferred advanced delegation

## Deliverables

Rich Authorization Request mappings, bounded opt-in chains, transaction/task and one-shot capability credentials, simulation/what-if and access certification. Global external trust/linking and device flow remain separately deferred decisions.

## Exit criteria

The review produces one explicit decision admitting a bounded next delegation design or retaining the disabled baseline.

## Required observations

Only after simpler model and explicit security approval: depth limits, full actors, no scope/audience/expiry widening, every hop audited; simulation cannot mint authority.

`systems/mandate/ess-inputs.yaml`, `docs/architecture/combined.md`, `docs/requirements.md` (all original phases and addendum requirements). Advanced delegation is deferred; other addendum requirements are mandatory.
